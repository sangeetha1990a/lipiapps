Summary: PokerRaja is a play-money poker game. The Service Provider does not require user accounts and does not operate a personal-information server. 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy applies to the PokerRaja mobile application (referred to as the "Application"), provided by Sangeetha (referred to as the "Service Provider").

PokerRaja is provided as a free, ad-supported application with optional in-app purchases. The Application is designed for Texas Hold'em and Omaha Hi-Lo poker gameplay against AI opponents and against other players over local WiFi or a mobile hotspot.

By using the Application, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information Collection and Use

PokerRaja is designed so that local multiplayer gameplay can take place between nearby devices without requiring an internet connection or a remote game server. The Application does not require users to create an online account.

2.1 Information We Do Not Collect for Gameplay

For gameplay purposes, PokerRaja does not require users to create an online account and does not collect or transmit the following information to our own servers:

  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Postal address
  • Precise GPS location
  • Personal profile information for online accounts
  • Social media account information
  • Contacts or address book
  • Photos, camera, or microphone data

Player names entered in the Application are used for gameplay and may be stored locally on the device. They may also be transmitted locally to other players' devices when required for a multiplayer game.

3. Local Storage on Your Device

PokerRaja stores certain information locally on your device to provide application functionality. This may include:

  • Player name, avatar, and profile settings
  • Virtual chip bankroll and purchased bankroll totals
  • VIP Premium purchase status and restoration state
  • Hand history and tournament history (for statistics and replay)
  • Achievements and progress
  • Daily ad-watch counters, free rebuy counters, and wheel spin counters
  • Game settings and preferences (sound, music, table theme, four-color deck, etc.)
  • Saved game state for resume functionality
  • Application language preference
  • Technical identifiers required for application functionality

This locally stored information is not transmitted to a PokerRaja remote server.

You can generally remove locally stored application data through the Android device settings or by uninstalling the Application. Some application settings or saved information may be lost when local application data is deleted. Virtual chips stored locally are permanently lost if you uninstall the Application or clear its data.

Third-party advertising providers may automatically collect or process certain information from the device when advertisements are requested or displayed. Depending on the applicable advertising configuration and user settings, this may include:

  • IP address

This information may be used by the advertising provider for purposes such as delivering, measuring, personalizing, and improving advertisements, detecting invalid activity, and maintaining advertising services.

The Service Provider does not directly receive or store users' payment card information or other payment credentials through PokerRaja.

For more information about how Google handles information in connection with advertising services, please review Google's applicable privacy information: https://policies.google.com/privacy

5. In-App Purchases

PokerRaja offers optional in-app purchases. Two types of purchases are available:

  • Consumable chip packs — one-time purchases that credit virtual chips to your in-game bankroll. Consumable purchases may be repeated.
  • VIP Premium — a one-time, non-consumable purchase that permanently unlocks additional features (ad-free play, doubled rewarded-ad rewards, free tournament entry up to a specified buy-in threshold, and WiFi multiplayer access). VIP Premium can be restored on reinstall through Google Play.

Payments and purchase processing are handled through Google Play's billing services. PokerRaja does not collect or store users' credit card numbers, debit card numbers, bank account information, or other payment credentials.

Google Play may process purchase-related information according to Google's terms and privacy policies. For information about Google Play's privacy practices, please visit: https://policies.google.com/privacy

6. Virtual Chip Economy

Virtual chips have no cash value.

PokerRaja uses virtual chips as an in-game currency for gameplay. Virtual chips:

  • Cannot be exchanged for real money, real-world goods, or cash prizes
  • Cannot be cashed out, sold, or transferred to another user
  • Cannot be redeemed outside the Application
  • Hold no monetary value in any currency
  • Are licensed to the user for in-game use only, not owned by the user
  • May be modified or lost through gameplay, technical events, or as required by applicable law

Upon Google Play's confirmation of a purchase, chips are credited to your in-game bankroll. In the rare event that the Application terminates unexpectedly between Google Play's confirmation and chip delivery, the Application's recovery system attempts to credit the purchase on your next launch. Consumable chip purchases are non-refundable once chips have been credited to your in-game bankroll, except as required by Google Play's refund policy or applicable law. Virtual chips that have been spent in gameplay cannot be recovered under any circumstance.

7. Local Multiplayer Communication

PokerRaja supports multiplayer poker games over the same local WiFi network or mobile hotspot, with up to 9 players at a single table.

During a multiplayer game, devices may exchange information required to operate the game, including:

  • Player names and avatar selections
  • Hole cards, board cards, and game state information
  • Betting actions, chip stacks, and pot information
  • Scores and hand results
  • Connection and synchronization information
  • Chat messages sent by players during the game

This information is exchanged between participating devices for the purpose of providing multiplayer gameplay. The Application does not operate a remote server that stores players' multiplayer game data.

Where supported by the Application, multiplayer data is transferred using encrypted communication. However, the security of the underlying WiFi network, mobile hotspot, or other local network is outside the control of the Service Provider.

8. Chat and User-Generated Content

PokerRaja provides an in-game chat feature for multiplayer sessions.

Messages entered into the multiplayer chat are transmitted between participating devices as part of the local multiplayer session. PokerRaja does not operate a remote server that stores multiplayer chat messages.

Users are responsible for the content they choose to send through the in-game chat feature.

13. Data Retention and Deletion

PokerRaja does not maintain user accounts or a remote database containing users' personal information for normal gameplay.

Information such as player settings, saved games, virtual chip balances, hand history, tournament history, achievements, and other application data may remain locally on the device until the user deletes the application data, uses an available in-app deletion/reset function, or uninstalls the Application.

Uninstalling PokerRaja deletes locally stored virtual chips, purchased bankroll records, hand history, achievements, and profile data. Virtual chips that have already been credited to your in-game bankroll cannot be recovered after uninstall.

If Google Play confirmed a purchase but the Application terminated before chip delivery (a rare technical failure), PokerRaja's recovery system may attempt to credit the purchase on reinstall, provided that (a) the pending-recovery record survives on your device — this typically requires Android auto-backup to be enabled — and (b) you reinstall the Application using the same Google Play account used for the original purchase. If auto-backup is disabled or the device is factory-reset, the pending-recovery record is lost.

VIP Premium ownership is managed by Google Play and can be restored on reinstall by signing in with the same Google account and using the "Restore Purchases" function within the Application.

Multiplayer game data and chat messages exchanged between devices are intended for the current local multiplayer session and are not stored by a PokerRaja remote server.

Third-party service providers, including advertising and payment providers, may retain information according to their own policies and legal requirements.

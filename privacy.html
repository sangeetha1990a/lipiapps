Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 18, 2026

LiPi Apps ("we", "our", or "us") operates mobile applications for Android and iOS platforms. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our apps.

Summary: We do not collect personal information directly. Our apps use third-party services (Google, Firebase) for ads, analytics, and crash reporting.

Apps Covered

This policy applies to the following applications:

  • Rummy Points — Entertainment
  • WD Viewer — Tools
  • WiFi Rummy Multiplayer — Games

Information We Collect

We do not collect personally identifiable information directly. However, our apps may use third-party services that collect information used to identify you:

  • Google Play Services
  • Google Analytics for Firebase
  • Google AdMob (for advertisements)
  • Firebase Crashlytics

App Permissions

Our apps may request the following permissions:

  • WiFi / Network: To enable multiplayer gaming over local network
  • Storage: To save game data and backups on your device
  • Internet: To display ads and enable online features
  • Nearby Devices: To connect with players on the same WiFi

How We Use Information

The information collected through third-party services is used to:

  • Provide and maintain our apps
  • Improve user experience
  • Display relevant advertisements
  • Analyze app performance and fix crashes
  • Detect and prevent technical issues

Data Storage & Security

Game data (scores, game history) is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted to our servers. We do not operate any server that stores your personal data.

We value your trust and use commercially acceptable means of protecting your data. However, no method of electronic storage is 100% secure.

Advertising

Our apps display advertisements through Google AdMob. AdMob may use device identifiers to show personalized ads. You can opt out of personalized advertising through your device settings.

Learn more: Google Ads Policy

In-App Purchases

Some apps offer in-app purchases (such as premium features). All transactions are processed securely by Google Play Billing. We do not have access to your payment information.

Children's Privacy

Our apps do not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

Third-Party Services

Please review the privacy policies of our third-party providers:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Firebase Privacy Policy
  • AdMob Privacy

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Uninstall our apps at any time
  • Clear app data from your device settings
  • Opt out of personalized ads
  • Contact us with privacy concerns
